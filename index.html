        Questions teams ask us

        Do I need a credit card to start?

        No. The Free plan needs only an email address, and you can stay on it as long as you like.

        Can I invite people outside my company?

        Yes. Share a link and add clients or freelancers to a single project without giving them access to the rest.

        Can I change plans later?

        You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing date.

        Is my data safe?

        Your tasks are encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export everything on the Business plan.
